# Code of conduct

logsite follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests,
Discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@rajpaynaik](https://github.com/rajpaynaik). Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer may remove content or block anyone who breaks this
code.
